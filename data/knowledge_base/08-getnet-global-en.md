# About Getnet

Getnet describes itself as a leading AI payments company in Latin America and Europe (Iberia), part of the Santander group. It helps businesses grow through an innovative, frictionless and secure payment platform that connects payments, data and finance.

## Key figures (2025)

- #1 LATAM acquirer by purchase volume and among the top 10 globally.
- Around €238bn total processed volume and about 10.5bn transactions processed in 2025.
- About 1.2 million active merchants; acquiring licence covering 35 countries.
- Presence in Brazil, Mexico, Chile, Argentina, Uruguay, Colombia, Spain and Portugal.

## Solutions

- **In-person payments**: card machines/POS, SoftPOS (tap on phone) and in-store solutions.
- **Online payments**: e-commerce checkout, payment links, APIs (e.g. Getnet SEP, a single e-commerce API for Brazil, Argentina, Mexico and Chile).
- **Omnichannel**: connect physical and digital channels, multicurrency and multilanguage.
- **Value added solutions**: fraud prevention, data insights, loyalty (Getnet Engage, launched in 2026), financial solutions.
- **Agentic commerce**: infrastructure that lets businesses accept payments initiated by AI agents.
- **Developers**: Get Code developer community and APIs.

## Segments

International companies (airlines, travel, mobility), SMEs and entrepreneurs, large enterprises, restaurants, health & beauty.
